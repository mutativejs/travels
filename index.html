NATURAL UPDATES, PRECISE HISTORY

Write like it’s mutable.
Travel like it’s immutable.

Travels uses Mutative to turn familiar draft mutations into immutable state and reversible patches. No reducer boilerplate, no manual inverse operations.

  • No-op updates stay out of history
  • Metadata labels every meaningful edit
  • Read-only controls fit any UI

ONE CORE, TWO OWNERSHIP MODELS

History that fits your architecture.

Let Travels own updates, or keep your existing store authoritative. The history contract stays explicit in both directions.

STATE-OWNING

createTravels()

Send updates through Travels and receive immutable states, patches, navigation, and controls.

Updates flow from your code through Travels to your UI. back() and forward() go from your UI to Travels.

Your updateTravelsYour UIback() · forward()
Own state with Travels
EXTERNALLY OWNED

createTravelJournal()

Record patches from an existing store or state machine, then delegate undo/redo through its commit path.

Your store commits and records patches in the journal, which drives your UI. back() and forward() are delegated back through your store’s commit path.

Your storeJournalYour UIback() delegates to your store
